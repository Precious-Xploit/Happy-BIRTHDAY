She Is Gold, Frankincense & Myrrh

Some people come into your life like a passing breeze.

You feel them for a moment, smile, and eventually, they become a memory.

And then there are those rare people who come into your life and somehow become home.

She is one of those people.

If I had to describe her, I wouldn't start with words like beautiful, kind, or supportive, even though she is all of those things.

I would describe her as gold.

Because gold is precious.

It doesn't lose its value because it is far away.

And somehow, no matter the distance between us, she has remained one of the most valuable people in my life.

She calls when I'm down.

Sometimes before I even say what's wrong, she already knows.

She listens.

She encourages.

She reminds me who I am when I temporarily forget.

And then comes her wisdom, the kind that feels less like advice from a friend and more like words from a mother who has lived a thousand lives.

There is one thing she said to me that I don't think I'll ever forget:

“Be gracious to yourself.”

Four simple words.

But they found a permanent home in my heart.

Because sometimes, we are so busy trying to become better, stronger, wiser, and everything everyone expects us to be, that we forget to be gentle with ourselves.

She reminded me.

And that is what she does.

She reminds me.

She reminds me that I am allowed to rest.

Allowed to make mistakes.

Allowed to grow slowly.

Allowed to be human.

If gold represents her worth, then frankincense represents her wisdom, the kind that rises quietly but fills every room.

And myrrh represents her presence, something deeply comforting, something that stays with you long after the conversation ends.

She may be far away, but distance has never been powerful enough to make her feel distant.

Because some people live beside you, and some people live within you.

She lives within me.

In the lessons she has taught me.

In the calls that lifted me.

In the laughter we've shared.

In the words I remember on difficult days.

And perhaps that's the most beautiful thing about her.

She doesn't have to be physically close to be deeply present.

She is the kind of friend who becomes family without asking for the title.

The kind of person you thank God for quietly.

The kind of person you hope life is just as gentle with as she has been with you.

So today, I celebrate more than a friend, a big sister.

I celebrate a woman who has been gold to my heart, frankincense to my wisdom, and myrrh to my soul.

And if anyone ever asks me what an amazing person looks like…

I will simply say:

She is you, and you are her.

Gold her worth
Frankincense her wisdom
Myrrh her presence
